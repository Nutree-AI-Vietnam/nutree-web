A medium Vietnamese pork sandwich (bánh mì thịt) usually has about **300–450 kcal**. Using values from the Vietnamese Food Composition Table, a sandwich made of 80 g of bread, 20 g of pâté and 40 g of chả lụa (Vietnamese pork roll) comes to about 320 kcal; spread butter or a rich sauce on it and it reaches about 400 kcal ([Vietnamese Food Composition Table, as cited by Tâm Anh General Hospital](https://tamanhhospital.vn/bang-calo-thuc-pham/), in Vietnamese). A bigger roll, more pâté, fatty meats or an added egg all push it higher.

This article breaks down banh mi calories part by part, shows how much a small and a large sandwich differ, and explains how to order banh mi when you're trying to lose weight. Every number below is an estimate: the real value depends on each stall's recipe and portion size.

## How many calories are in a banh mi?

There is no single number for every banh mi, because each stall uses a different roll, different fillings and a different amount of butter and sauce. The closest estimate comes from adding up the parts. The table below is an **illustrative example** of a medium sandwich, using the per-100 g values of the Vietnamese Food Composition Table: bread 249 kcal, pâté 326 kcal, chả lụa 136 kcal ([Tâm Anh General Hospital](https://tamanhhospital.vn/bang-calo-thuc-pham/), in Vietnamese).

| Part | Serving (example) | Estimated calories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bread roll | 80 g | 80 × 2.49 ≈ 199 kcal |
| Pâté | 20 g | 20 × 3.26 ≈ 65 kcal |
| Chả lụa | 40 g | 40 × 1.36 ≈ 54 kcal |
| **Total (no butter or sauce)** | | **about 318 kcal** |
| Butter or rich sauce | about 10 g of fat | about 90 kcal |
| **Total (with butter or sauce)** | | **about 408 kcal** |

Each gram of fat gives about 9 kcal ([FAO, 2003](https://www.fao.org/4/y5022e/y5022e00.htm)), so just 10 g of fat from butter, mayonnaise or spring onion oil is about 90 kcal. Cucumber, pickles, coriander and chilli add very few calories compared with the rest.

So if you don't know the recipe, **about 300–450 kcal for a medium banh mi** is a sensible starting point. Add to it when the roll is bigger or has extra fillings.

## Where do the calories in banh mi come from?

A pork banh mi has four main parts: the bread, the meat filling (pâté, chả lụa, cold cuts, char siu and so on), the butter or sauce, and the vegetables and pickles. Each brings a different macronutrient: the bread is mostly carbohydrate, the pork roll and meats bring protein, and the pâté, butter and sauce carry most of the fat.

### The bread: the biggest source of calories

In the example above, the bread accounts for about 199 of the 318 kcal, nearly two thirds of the calories before any butter or sauce. USDA data for French bread (“Bread, french or vienna”) gives a similar result: about 272 kcal, 10.75 g of protein and 51.88 g of carbohydrate per 100 g ([USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/food-details/172675/nutrients)). For an 80 g roll:

| Source | Calories per 100 g | 80 g roll |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vietnamese Food Composition Table, “Bánh mì” | 249 kcal | about 199 kcal |
| USDA, “Bread, french or vienna” | 272 kcal | about 218 kcal |

By the USDA figures, an 80 g roll has about 41.5 g of carbohydrate and 8.6 g of protein. In other words, the bread alone is about one meal's serving of starch.

### Pâté, chả lụa and other meats

- **Pâté** has about 326 kcal per 100 g, more than twice as much as chả lụa ([Tâm Anh General Hospital](https://tamanhhospital.vn/bang-calo-thuc-pham/), in Vietnamese). A thick layer of pâté is the quickest way to add calories to a sandwich.
- **Chả lụa** has about 136 kcal per 100 g, a fairly light filling compared with pâté.
- **Cold cuts, char siu and pork belly** usually carry more fat than chả lụa, so a “special” banh mi (bánh mì đặc biệt) with several meats usually has more calories than one with pork roll only.

## Small, medium and large: how many calories apart?

Portion size decides most of the total. The table below is an **illustrative example** that counts only the bread, pâté and chả lụa, using the Vietnamese Food Composition Table, without butter or sauce:

| Size (example) | Bread | Pâté | Chả lụa | Estimated calories |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 60 g | 15 g | 30 g | about 239 kcal |
| Medium | 80 g | 20 g | 40 g | about 318 kcal |
| Large | 100 g | 30 g | 60 g | about 428 kcal |

Going from a medium to a large sandwich adds about 110 kcal, before butter and sauce. If you often order the special or add an egg, count that difference in your daily total.

<Callout type="note" title="Banh mi and salt">
Pâté, chả lụa, cold cuts, soy sauce and chilli sauce all contain salt. WHO recommends that adults eat less than 5 g of salt a day ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/salt-reduction)), counting the salt in processed foods and sauces. If you're cutting down on salt, especially with high blood pressure or kidney disease, ask your doctor what amount suits you.
</Callout>

## Is banh mi fattening?

No single food makes you gain weight on its own. According to WHO, the basic cause of overweight and obesity is an imbalance between the energy you take in and the energy you use ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/obesity-and-overweight)). A banh mi at about 300–450 kcal can fit into a weight-loss plan, as long as your total calories for the day stay below what your body uses.

Banh mi has a few points in its favour: it's easy to take with you, it has protein from the pork roll and meats, and it comes with some vegetables and pickles. The things to watch are big rolls, lots of pâté, butter and rich sauce, and the salt in processed fillings. A banh mi also has far fewer vegetables than a noodle soup such as beef pho (see [pho calories](/en/blog/pho-calories)), so you may get hungry again sooner if you eat one on its own.

## How to eat banh mi when you're losing weight

You don't have to give up banh mi. A few small changes when you order cut calories while keeping it tasty:

1. **Choose a small or medium roll.** The bread is the biggest source of calories.
2. **Ask for less pâté, butter and sauce.** These are the fattiest parts.
3. **Pick chả lụa, chicken or egg** instead of cold cuts, char siu or pork belly.
4. **Ask for extra cucumber, pickles and herbs** to add vegetables for very few calories.
5. **Go easy on chilli and soy sauce** if you're watching your salt.
6. **Add vegetables or fruit on the side** to make the meal more filling and balanced.

<Callout type="tip" title="Ordering banh mi on a diet">
“A medium banh mi with chả lụa, a little pâté, no butter, extra cucumber and pickles” is a simple order that keeps breakfast filling and lighter in calories.
</Callout>

## Tracking calories when you buy banh mi

The hardest part of buying banh mi from a street cart is not knowing how much the roll weighs or how much pâté and butter go in. A few habits help you estimate more closely:

- **Remember your usual roll and filling** at the place you buy from most, and use the same estimate each time so you can compare days.
- **Log the extras separately**, such as egg, butter, sauce or extra meat, because they change the total the most.
- **Log the drink too**, such as a morning iced milk coffee or milk tea.
- **Use a calorie-tracking app**: Nutree estimates calories and macros from a photo of your meal, a barcode or a dish name you type, such as “bánh mì chả lụa”, then adjusts your targets as you progress.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many calories are in a banh mi?

A medium pork banh mi usually has about 300–450 kcal. For example, a sandwich with 80 g of bread, 20 g of pâté and 40 g of chả lụa has about 318 kcal, and about 400 kcal with butter or a rich sauce ([Tâm Anh General Hospital](https://tamanhhospital.vn/bang-calo-thuc-pham/), in Vietnamese).

### How many calories are in a plain banh mi roll?

According to the Vietnamese Food Composition Table, bread has about 249 kcal per 100 g ([Tâm Anh General Hospital](https://tamanhhospital.vn/bang-calo-thuc-pham/), in Vietnamese), so an 80 g roll has about 199 kcal. For a bigger or smaller roll, scale by its real weight.

### Which has more calories, pâté or chả lụa?

Weight for weight, pâté has more: about 326 against 136 kcal per 100 g ([Tâm Anh General Hospital](https://tamanhhospital.vn/bang-calo-thuc-pham/), in Vietnamese). A sandwich heavy on pâté usually has more calories than one filled mostly with chả lụa.

### Is banh mi for breakfast fattening?

When you eat matters less than your total energy for the day. If the banh mi fits your calorie target, it won't make you gain weight ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/obesity-and-overweight)). A medium roll with little pâté and butter, plus some vegetables, makes a lighter breakfast.
